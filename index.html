Dr. Amylia Bal

Specialist Prosthodontist

BDS, DClinDent (Pros), FAANZP

DEN0001925433

Dr. Amylia Bal graduated from the University of Adelaide with a Bachelor of Dental Surgery, and her early career took her to private practices in regional and metropolitan NSW. She developed a keen interest in holistic dental rehabilitation that led to a Doctor of Clinical Dentistry in Prosthodontics at the University of Sydney.

Dr. Bal holds a Clinical Lecturer position at the University of Sydney, established and continues to direct the Sydney University ITI Study Club, and is the current President for the Australian Prosthodontic Society (APS) NSW Branch. She is a Fellow of the Academy of Australian and New Zealand Prosthodontists (AANZP).

Outside of her professional endeavours, Dr Bal enjoys travelling and the great outdoors, bringing a well-rounded perspective to her work.
